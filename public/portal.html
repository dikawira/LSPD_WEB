LSPD Department Guide

A guide to the purpose, responsibilities, and functions of every office, bureau, and division within the Los Santos Police Department.

Department Reference

This page explains the role of each organizational unit. Each Bureau supervises its listed Divisions, while offices under the Chief's Office provide executive coordination and internal support. All units operate under the LSPD chain of command and applicable department procedures.

What Is Executive Leadership?

Executive Leadership adalah tingkat kepemimpinan dan tata kelola tertinggi di LSPD. Bagian ini menetapkan arah departemen, menentukan prioritas, mengawasi pimpinan, dan memastikan setiap Bureau serta Division memiliki tujuan dan pertanggungjawaban yang jelas. Executive Leadership tidak menjalankan setiap tugas lapangan secara langsung; pelaksanaan sehari-hari didelegasikan melalui Chief's Office, Office of Operations, Office of Support Services, dan para pimpinan unit.

Executive Leadership & Chief's Office

Struktur kepemimpinan, pengambilan keputusan, koordinasi operasi, dan dukungan internal

Board of Commissioner

Executive Oversight

Provides the department's highest level of oversight and strategic direction. Reviews senior leadership, supports accountability, and guides major departmental policy and governance decisions.

Chief's Office

Executive Command

Serves as the central leadership office of LSPD. Establishes departmental priorities, issues executive directives, supervises senior command, and oversees the overall direction of the department.

Office of Operations

Operational Command

Coordinates department-wide field operations, aligns bureau activity with command priorities, monitors operational readiness, and reviews the effectiveness of patrol and specialized units.

Office of Support Services

Administrative Support

Provides the department with internal administrative and logistical support, including records, equipment, facilities, and resources needed by LSPD offices, bureaus, and divisions.

Special Operations Bureau

Specialized operational and tactical support

Special Operations Bureau

Bureau Command

Oversees specialized units assigned to operations that require advanced capabilities, dedicated planning, and coordinated tactical or technical support.

Air Support Division

Division

Provides aviation support to patrol and special operations through aerial observation, search assistance, situational awareness, and coordination with ground units.

SWAT Division

Division

Provides specialized tactical response for high-risk incidents, including hostage situations, barricaded subjects, and other events requiring advanced training and equipment.

Speed Hunter Division

Division

Focuses on traffic enforcement and dangerous driving, including vehicle pursuits and incidents involving excessive speed, in accordance with department pursuit policy.

Patrol Division

Primary uniformed field operations

Patrol Division

Field Operations

Acts as the department's primary uniformed response unit. Handles routine patrol, calls for service, traffic stops, initial response to incidents, and general law enforcement in the city.

Investigations Bureau

Criminal case development and investigative coordination

Investigations Bureau

Bureau Command

Supervises the department's investigative functions and coordinates the development of criminal cases across the investigative divisions under its authority.

Criminal Investigations Division

Division

Investigates criminal cases by gathering and reviewing evidence, interviewing involved persons, identifying suspects, and preparing case reports and investigative documentation.

Gang & Narcotics Division

Division

Focuses on investigations involving criminal gangs, narcotics activity, and suspected distribution networks, using evidence-led case development and coordination with other units.

Administrative Services Bureau

Administrative services and public communication

Administrative Services Bureau

Bureau Command

Oversees administrative and public-facing services that support departmental operations, including licensing services, official communication, and community relations.

Firearms Licensing Division

Division

Receives and reviews firearms licence applications, manages renewals and licence records, and processes eligibility checks under the department's roleplay licensing rules.

Media & Relations Division

Division

Manages LSPD's public information and media presence, publishes official announcements, coordinates press briefings, and maintains relations with community and government partners.

Professional Standards Bureau

Personnel development, training, and accountability

Professional Standards Bureau

Bureau Command

Promotes professional conduct and departmental accountability while overseeing personnel development, training standards, and internal review functions.

Human Resource & Training Division

Division

Supports recruitment and personnel administration, coordinates onboarding and academy processes, schedules training, and helps members maintain and develop their professional skills.

Internal Affairs Division

Division

Reviews allegations of member misconduct, conducts internal inquiries, documents findings, and submits recommendations through the appropriate command channels.

LSPD · Personnel Classification

Rank Categories

Klasifikasi jabatan, tanggung jawab, dan kelompok pangkat personel LSPD.

Rank Classification

Rank Category membantu anggota memahami fungsi dan tingkat tanggung jawab setiap kelompok jabatan. Pangkat menunjukkan posisi dalam hierarki, sedangkan kategori menjelaskan kelompok fungsi atau penugasan yang melekat pada pangkat tersebut.

HIGH COMMAND

EXECUTIVE LEADERSHIP

Jajaran pimpinan tertinggi yang menetapkan kebijakan, arah strategis, prioritas departemen, dan pengawasan organisasi. High Command memastikan seluruh Bureau dan Division berjalan sesuai arahan Chief's Office serta tata kelola LSPD.

Ranks includedCommissioner · Chief of Police · Assistant Chief · Deputy Chief · Commander

COMMAND

UNIT MANAGEMENT

Jajaran komando yang memimpin Division atau fungsi operasional. Mereka mengelola personel, menerjemahkan kebijakan menjadi rencana kerja, memantau kinerja, dan memastikan prosedur dijalankan.

Ranks includedCaptain · Lieutenant

SUPERVISOR

FIELD SUPERVISION

Jajaran pengawasan lapangan yang membina anggota, memberikan arahan, memeriksa pelaksanaan tugas, membantu menangani kendala operasional, dan mengevaluasi kinerja personel.

Ranks includedSergeant

FIELD TRAINING OFFICER (FTO)

TRAINING ASSIGNMENT

Petugas instruktur yang bertanggung jawab membimbing, melatih, dan mengevaluasi Cadet / Probationary. FTO membantu anggota baru memahami SOP, komunikasi radio, prosedur patroli, dokumentasi, dan standar profesional LSPD.

ClassificationFTO · Penugasan khusus

DETECTIVE

INVESTIGATIVE RANK

Satu pangkat investigasi pada Criminal Investigations Division (CID). Detective menangani pengembangan perkara, pengumpulan bukti, wawancara, analisis informasi, dan dokumentasi investigasi.

Rank includedDetective

OFFICER

FIELD PERSONNEL

Personel penegak hukum operasional yang menjalankan tugas kepolisian di lapangan, termasuk patroli, respons panggilan, traffic stop, pelayanan masyarakat, dan penegakan hukum sesuai kewenangan.

Ranks includedPolice Officer III · Police Officer II · Police Officer I

CADET

ENTRY / PROBATIONARY

Personel dalam masa pendidikan dan pelatihan dasar kepolisian. Cadet mengikuti orientasi, mempelajari kebijakan dan SOP, serta menjalani evaluasi sebelum memperoleh penugasan penuh sebagai officer.

ClassificationCadet / Probationary

FTO merupakan penugasan pelatih khusus, bukan pangkat. Detective merupakan satu pangkat investigasi formal pada Criminal Investigations Division (CID), tanpa tingkatan I, II, atau III.
